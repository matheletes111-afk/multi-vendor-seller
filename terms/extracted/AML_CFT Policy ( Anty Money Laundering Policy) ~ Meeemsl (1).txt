ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) &
COUNTER FINANCING OF TERRORISM (CFT) POLICY
Introduction
This Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter Financing of Terrorism (CFT) Policy establishes the framework adopted by Meeemsl.com (the “Platform”), a multi-seller eCommerce marketplace operating in Sierra Leone, offering:
Online sale of products by multiple sellers
Service booking through mobile applications
Digital payment processing via integrated payment gateways
The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the Platform is not used for:
Money laundering
Terrorist financing
Fraudulent or illegal transactions
This policy aligns with international and local regulatory expectations, including:
Financial Action Task Force (FATF Recommendations)
Bank of Sierra Leone (BSL Guidelines)
Financial Intelligence Unit Sierra Leone (FIU-SL)
Relevant Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing laws of Sierra Leone
Scope
This policy applies to:
Customers (buyers using mobile/web apps)
Sellers (product vendors)
Service Providers (service listing & booking entities)
Employees and management of Meeemsl.com
Third-party partners, including payment gateway providers
Risk-Based Approach (RBA)
Meeemsl.com adopts a risk-based approach to identify, assess, and mitigate AML/CFT risks.
Risk Categories
Customer Risk: Anonymous users, high-value transactions, frequent refunds
Seller/Service Provider Risk: New or unverified vendors, unusual business models
Geographical Risk: High-risk jurisdictions identified by FATF
Transaction Risk: High-value orders, rapid transactions, abnormal patterns
Risk Levels
Low Risk
Medium Risk
High Risk (Enhanced Due Diligence required)
Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & KYC
Customers (Buyers)
Registration via mobile number/email with OTP verification
Monitoring of transaction patterns
Additional verification for high-value transactions
Sellers & Service Providers (Mandatory KYC)
Before onboarding, the Platform shall collect:
Full legal name / business name
Government-issued ID (passport, national ID, etc.)
Business registration documents (if applicable)
Bank account details
Address proof
Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
EDD shall be applied where:
High-risk jurisdictions are involved
Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) are identified
Unusual transaction patterns occur
Payment Controls
All payments must be processed via approved payment gateways
No direct peer-to-peer transfers allowed outside the platform
Refunds must be routed through original payment methods
Cash transactions (if any) must be strictly limited and monitored
Transaction Monitoring
Meeemsl.com shall implement automated and manual monitoring systems to detect:
Unusual transaction patterns
Multiple high-value transactions in short periods
Rapid buying and refund cycles
Suspicious service bookings
Red Flags Include:
Mismatch between user profile and transaction value
Repeated failed payment attempts
Multiple accounts linked to same identity
Transactions involving high-risk countries
Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR)
Any suspicious activity shall be reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit Sierra Leone ● Reports must include:
○ Customer details
○ Transaction history
○ Nature of suspicion
Employees must escalate concerns to the Compliance Officer immediately
Record Keeping
Meeemsl.com shall maintain records for a minimum of 5 years, including:
Customer identification data
Transaction logs
Payment records
Communication records
All records must be securely stored and accessible for regulatory review.
Sanctions Screening
The Platform shall ensure that:
Users are screened against international sanctions lists (e.g., UN lists)
Transactions involving sanctioned individuals/entities are blocked
Continuous monitoring is performed for updates
Prohibited Activities
The Platform strictly prohibits:
Sale of illegal goods/services
Transactions linked to terrorism or criminal activity
Use of fake identities or documents
Circumvention of payment systems
Violations will result in:
Immediate suspension
Account termination
Reporting to authorities
Roles & Responsibilities
Compliance Officer
Oversees AML/CFT implementation
Reports suspicious activities
Ensures regulatory compliance
Management
Approves AML policies
Allocates resources for compliance
Employees
Follow AML procedures
Report suspicious activities
Training & Awareness
Regular AML/CFT training for staff
Awareness programs for sellers and service providers
Updates on regulatory changes
Data Protection & Confidentiality
Customer data must be handled securely
AML-related information must remain confidential
Data sharing only with authorized regulators
Technology & Security Measures
Secure backend systems (e.g., Laravel APIs, MySQL database)
Encrypted payment processing
Fraud detection mechanisms
Audit logs for all transactions
Audit & Compliance Review
Periodic internal audits
External compliance checks (if required)
Continuous improvement of AML systems
Non-Compliance & Penalties
Failure to comply with this policy may result in:
Account suspension or termination
Legal action under Sierra Leone laws
Regulatory penalties
Policy Review
This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon:
Regulatory updates
Business model changes
New risk identification

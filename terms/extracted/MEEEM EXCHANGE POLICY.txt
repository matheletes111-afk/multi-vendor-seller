MEEEM EXCHANGE POLICY
Introduction
This Exchange Policy governs product exchanges made through MEEEM (“Platform”).
The Platform operates as a multi-vendor marketplace, and all exchange decisions are primarily defined and handled by the respective vendors.
SCOPE
This policy applies to:
Physical products purchase from vendors
Items eligible for replacement or exchange
Note: Services booked through the platform are not eligible for exchange, but may be rescheduled or cancelled as per the service provider’s policy
ROLE OF THE PLATFORM
The Platform acts as a facilitator between customers and vendors
Vendors are responsible for:
○ Accepting or rejecting exchange requests
○ Defining exchange conditions
○ Processing replacement items
The Platform may assist in dispute resolution if required.
EXCHANGE ELIGIBILITY
A product may be eligible for exchange under the following conditions:
Valid Reasons for Exchange
Defective or damaged product
Wrong product delivered
Size/variant mismatch (if allowed by vendor)
Missing parts or accessories
Conditions for Exchange
To qualify for exchange:
Request must be raised within [X days] (typically 3–7 days) or as mentioned by the vendor
Product must be:
○ Unused
○ In original packaging
○ With all tags, labels, and accessories intact
Proof may be required (images/videos)
Non-Exchangeable Items
Products may NOT be eligible for exchange if:
Marked as “non-exchangeable” by the vendor
Used, damaged, or altered by the customer
Missing original packaging or accessories
Personalized or custom-made items
Perishable goods (if applicable)
Exchange Process
Step 1: Request Submission
Customer raises an exchange request via:
● Mobile app
Website dashboard
Step 2: Vendor Review
Vendor reviews the request
May request additional proof
Step 3: Approval / Rejection
If approved → exchange initiated
If rejected → reason provided
Step 4: Return & Replacement
Customer returns product (pickup/drop-off)
Vendor ships replacement product
Logistics & Shipping
● Return shipping may be:
○ Covered by vendor (for defective/wrong items) ○ Paid by customer (for size/choice-based exchange) ● Replacement shipping timelines:
○ Typically 5–10 business days
Exchange Limits
Exchange is typically allowed once per order/item
Multiple exchanges for the same product may not be permitted
Price Difference Handling
Customer pays the difference If Replacement Product Costs Less
● Difference may be:
○ Refunded
○ Provided as store credit (as per vendor policy)
10. Service Exchange / Rescheduling
For service bookings:
● Exchanges are not applicable ● Rescheduling may be allowed if:
○ Requested within defined time
○ Approved by service provider
11. Vendor Responsibilities
Vendors must:
Clearly define exchange policies
Process requests within a reasonable timeframe
Maintain product quality and accuracy
Failure to comply may result in:
Penalties
Suspension from the platform
12. Platform Intervention
The Platform may intervene if:
Vendor is unresponsive
Dispute arises
Policy is violated
Actions may include:
Mediation
Escalation review
Temporary restrictions on vendor
13. Abuse of Exchange Policy
The Platform reserves the right to:
Reject fraudulent or abusive requests
Suspend accounts involved in misuse
14. Policy Transparency
All vendors must:
Display exchange policy clearly on product pages
Ensure customers accept terms before purchase
15. Governing Law
This policy shall be governed by the laws of Sierra Leone.

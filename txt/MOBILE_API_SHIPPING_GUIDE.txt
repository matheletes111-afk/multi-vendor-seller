1. OVERVIEW & CALCULATION FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The backend shipping engine calculates multi-vendor delivery charges dynamically based on 3 distinct components:

  1) Weight-based Shipping Fee (weightShippingFee)
  2) Dimension-based Shipping Fee (dimensionShippingFee)
  3) Region-based Shipping Fee (regionShippingFee)

ADDITIVE & PER-SELLER PACKAGING FORMULA:
- Items from DIFFERENT sellers are packaged and shipped independently.
- Cart items are grouped by `sellerId`.
- Each seller's total delivery charge is calculated as:
  
    Seller Delivery Fee = Weight Fee + Dimension Fee + Region Fee

- Total Order Shipping Fee = Sum of all Seller Delivery Fees:
  
    Total Order Shipping = Seller A Fee + Seller B Fee + ...

NULL & ZERO WEIGHT/DIMENSION/REGION RULE:
- Missing, null, or 0 weight/dimensions are treated as 0 kg / 0 cm³.
- They are NOT skipped and do NOT automatically default to 0/FREE.
- Instead, 0 kg / 0 cm³ is looked up in the Admin-configured tier table (e.g. 0-5 kg tier or base tier) to return the Admin's configured delivery fee.
- If address region is null/unspecified or unlisted, it maps to "Other" region and applies the Admin "Other" region delivery surcharge (or fallback first region charge).

3. MOBILE ADDRESS FORM & MAP REGION RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When adding or updating a delivery address (via POST/PATCH /mobileapi/customer/checkout/addresses),
the app MUST present a dropdown for the 'state' field containing Sierra Leone's
Administrative Regions + Fallback:

  - Eastern Province
  - Northern Province
  - North West Province
  - Southern Province
  - Western Area
  - Other                      <-- Fallback region for custom/other destinations

MAP & REVERSE GEOCODING REGION EXTRACTION:
- When a customer picks a location on the Map or uses GPS reverse-geocoding (lat/lng):
  1) Extract the administrative region or city from the geocoding response.
  2) Map major cities/areas to standard regions (e.g. Freetown -> Western Area, Kenema -> Eastern Province, Bo -> Southern Province, Makeni -> Northern Province).
  3) If the geocoded location or city does not match any of the 5 main provinces, pass `state: "Other"`.

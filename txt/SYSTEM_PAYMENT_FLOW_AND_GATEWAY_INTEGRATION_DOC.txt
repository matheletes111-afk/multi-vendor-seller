1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MEEEM is an enterprise multi-vendor marketplace connecting customers with merchants 
across five core sectors:
  • Physical E-Commerce Products (Single & multi-seller shopping carts)
  • Professional & Home Services (Appointments & fixed-price bookings)
  • Hotel & Hospitality Reservations (Nightly room bookings)
  • Restaurant & Cloud Kitchen Food Delivery
  • Merchant Digital Ecosystem (Monthly/annual subscriptions & CPC advertising)

PAYMENT PRINCIPLE:
  • All customer purchases are 100% PREPAID online via integrated payment gateways.
  • Customer funds are securely locked in a central ESCROW account until the order 
    or service is verified as DELIVERED / COMPLETED.
  • When delivery is confirmed, the platform automatically splits and disburses 
    funds to the seller, delivery boy (rider), and platform commission account.

2. THE 3-ACCOUNT FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
To maintain strict escrow compliance, transparent accounting, and clean tax 
segregation, the platform operates three dedicated corporate bank accounts:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT 1: ESCROW & ORDER RECONCILIATION ACCOUNT (Holding / Transit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose:
  - Receives 100% of customer payments for all product orders, service bookings,
    hotel stays, and restaurant food orders via online payment gateways.
• Status:
  - Funds in Account 1 are strictly in-transit / escrow. They do NOT belong to the
    platform or the seller until goods or services are fulfilled.
• Payout Triggers:
  - Once an item or service is DELIVERED / COMPLETED, funds in Account 1 are released:
    a) Seller Net Share -> Disbursed to Seller's Bank BBAN or Mobile Money Wallet.
    b) Delivery Boy (Rider) Fee -> Disbursed to Rider's Mobile Money Wallet.
    c) Platform Commission -> Transferred to Account 2.
• Refunds:
  - If an unfulfilled order or booking is cancelled before delivery, a full refund 
    is issued directly from Account 1 back to the customer's payment source.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT 2: PLATFORM COMMISSION & OPERATIONS ACCOUNT (Net Revenue)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose:
  - Receives the platform's net commission cuts swept from Account 1 after 
    verified fulfillment.
• Commission Examples:
  - Physical Products : 9% – 15% (category or merchant negotiated rate)
  - Hotel Bookings   : 10% – 19%
  - Restaurant/Food  : 10% – 20%
  - Services         : 10% – 15%
• Status:
  - Legally recognized gross operational revenue of the business used for operations,
    corporate tax, salaries, and expansion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT 3: MERCHANT ADVERTISING & SUBSCRIPTION ACCOUNT (Direct Revenue)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose:
  - Receives direct payments from merchants for:
    a) Seller Subscription Plans (FREE, STANDARD, PREMIUM monthly/annual tiers).
    b) Sponsored Banner & CPC (Cost-Per-Click) Advertising Wallet Top-ups.
• Status:
  - Immediate, 100% platform income. No escrow holds, no seller splits, and no 
    delivery boy involvement.
  - Payments route directly from the gateway into Account 3 upon checkout.

3. SYSTEM TRANSACTION & SETTLEMENT FLOWS (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLOW A: CUSTOMER CHECKOUT & ESCROW DEPOSIT (ALL VERTICALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Customer adds items/services to cart (Products, Services, Hotels, or Food).
2. Customer applies a promotional discount coupon (if available).
3. System calculates total payable amount:
   `Total = Items Subtotal + Taxes (GST) + Delivery Fee - Coupon Discount`
4. Customer selects online payment method (Card, Mobile Money, Bank Transfer).
5. Payment gateway charges customer and settles 100% of funds into ACCOUNT 1.
6. System receives payment success confirmation via webhook:
   - Order marked as "PAID" / "PROCESSING".
   - Funds are locked in Account 1 (Escrow). No payouts released yet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLOW B: PRODUCT ORDER FULFILLMENT & MULTI-VENDOR SPLIT ON DELIVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO: Customer orders from 2 different sellers in one checkout:
          - Package 1: Seller A items ($50 + $5 delivery fee)
          - Package 2: Seller B items ($50 + $5 delivery fee)
          - Total paid into Account 1 = $110.

1. Package Separation:
   - Seller A and Seller B receive independent order fulfillment lines.
   - Separate delivery boys (Rider A and Rider B) are dispatched for each package.

2. Milestone Delivery of Package 1 (Seller A):
   - Rider A delivers Package A to the customer and verifies the Customer Delivery OTP.
   - System immediately executes the Split Engine for Package 1:
     • Seller A Net Share: ($50 Item Price - Commission - Seller Coupon Share)
       -> Released to Seller A's withdrawable balance.
     • Rider A Delivery Fee: $5 shipping fee released to Rider A's earnings wallet.
     • Platform Commission: Accrued in Account 1, scheduled for transfer to ACCOUNT 2.
   - Escrow Status: The remaining $55 for Package 2 stays safely locked in Account 1.

3. Milestone Delivery of Package 2 (Seller B):
   - Rider B delivers Package B (even hours or days later) and verifies Delivery OTP.
   - Split Engine executes for Package 2:
     • Seller B Net Share -> Released to Seller B.
     • Rider B Delivery Fee -> Released to Rider B.
     • Platform Commission -> Scheduled for transfer to ACCOUNT 2.
   - Parent Order Status: All packages delivered -> Order marked "COMPLETED".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLOW C: PROFESSIONAL SERVICE BOOKING FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Customer books a home/professional service (e.g. Electrical Repair $100 + $15 GST).
2. Customer pays online ($115) -> Deposited into ACCOUNT 1. Booking marked "CONFIRMED".
3. Service provider attends the scheduled appointment and completes the work.
4. Customer confirms completion with OTP / digital signature -> Status: "COMPLETED".
5. Split Engine executes:
   • Platform Commission (e.g. 10% = $11.50) -> Transferred to ACCOUNT 2.
   • Service Provider Net ($103.50) -> Released for payout from Account 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLOW D: HOTEL ROOM RESERVATION FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Guest reserves a hotel room for 3 nights ($300 total).
2. Online payment ($300) collected into ACCOUNT 1. Booking marked "CONFIRMED".
3. Funds remain locked in Account 1 until the guest checks in and completes stay.
4. Upon verified checkout:
   • Hotel Seller Net Share (e.g. 85% = $255) -> Released to Hotel Seller balance.
   • Platform Commission (e.g. 15% = $45) -> Transferred to ACCOUNT 2.
5. Cancellation Protection: If guest cancels within free cancellation window, 100% 
   refund is returned from Account 1 back to the guest. Unearned money is never sent 
   to the hotel seller.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLOW E: RESTAURANT FOOD ORDER FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Customer orders meals from Restaurant ($40 food + $5 delivery fee = $45).
2. Payment collected into ACCOUNT 1. Restaurant prepares food.
3. Food delivery rider picks up order and delivers to customer via OTP verification.
4. Upon delivery confirmation:
   • Restaurant Net Share ($40 - Commission) -> Released to Restaurant.
   • Delivery Boy Fee ($5) -> Released to Rider.
   • Platform Commission -> Transferred to ACCOUNT 2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLOW F: MERCHANT ADVERTISING & SUBSCRIPTION CHECKOUT (DIRECT TO ACCOUNT 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Merchant selects a monthly/annual Subscription Plan or funds a CPC Ad Campaign.
2. Merchant can apply a promotional coupon (e.g. 20% off).
3. Merchant completes online checkout via payment gateway.
4. Payment gateway settles funds directly into OWNER ACCOUNT 3.
5. Webhook confirms payment:
   • Subscription is instantly activated for 30/365 days.
   • Or Ad campaign budget is funded and goes live.
   • ZERO escrow holds. 100% immediate platform revenue.

4. WHAT WE REQUIRE FROM THE PAYMENT GATEWAY / FINTECH PARTNER
================================================================================
To integrate any payment gateway (e.g. Flot, Stripe, Orange Money, AfriMoney, 
MonCash, Zenith Bank, Paystack, Flutterwave), the partner must support:

1. DUAL SETTLEMENT DESTINATIONS (MULTI-ACCOUNT LINKING):
   • Gateway must support routing checkout transactions based on payment context:
     - Customer Marketplace Orders -> Settle directly into OWNER ACCOUNT 1 (Escrow).
     - Merchant Subscriptions & Ads -> Settle directly into OWNER ACCOUNT 3 (Revenue).

2. MULTI-CHANNEL PAYMENT ACCEPTANCE:
   • Visa & Mastercard (Local and International debit/credit cards).
   • Mobile Money Wallets (Orange Money, Africell AfriMoney, etc.).
   • Bank Direct Debit / Account-to-Account rails.
   • QR Code and In-App Wallets.

3. WEBHOOK EVENT NOTIFICATIONS:
   • Real-time server-to-server POST webhooks on payment events:
     - `PAYMENT.SUCCESS`
     - `PAYMENT.FAILED`
     - `REFUND.COMPLETED`
   • Cryptographic signature verification (HMAC-SHA256 or RSA key pair) to guarantee
     authenticity and prevent fraud.
   • Idempotent event processing with unique transaction reference IDs.

4. AUTOMATED OUTBOUND DISBURSEMENT / BULK PAYOUT API:
   • API capability to trigger payouts from Account 1 to third parties:
     - Direct transfers to commercial bank BBANs (for seller withdrawals).
     - Instant transfers to Mobile Money Wallets (for rider earnings).
     - Internal sweeping transfers to Account 2 (for platform commissions).

5. REVERSIBLE REFUND CAPABILITY:
   • Secure refund API allowing automated or admin-approved refunds from Account 1
     directly back to customer cards or mobile money wallets for cancelled orders.

7. SUMMARY FOR CLIENT DECISION & GATEWAY IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================
1. The business is 100% PREPAID ONLINE:
   - Eliminates cash handling losses, float discrepancies, and theft risk.
   - Customers pay upfront; money is held in Account 1 until delivery OTP verification.

2. Settlement on Delivery:
   - When order delivered: Seller gets item money, delivery boy gets delivery fee, 
     and platform gets commission.
   - In multi-seller carts, each seller's package is paid out independently as soon
     as their package is delivered.

3. Ads & Subscriptions:
   - Merchants pay upfront directly into Account 3 with coupon discount support.

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & ONBOARDING ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The MEEEM multi-vendor marketplace supports four distinct commercial vendor
verticals alongside an internal logistics delivery fleet:

1. PRODUCT SELLERS (/product-seller)
   Retailers, distributors, and manufacturers listing physical goods with
   inventory, variants, and weight-based shipping.

2. SERVICE PROVIDERS (/service-seller)
   Professional tradespeople, home maintenance technicians, and freelancers
   providing on-demand or appointment-based services.

3. HOTEL HOSTS / HOTELIERS (/hotel-seller)
   Hotels, resorts, guesthouses, and homestays offering room reservations,
   check-in/check-out management, and seasonal tariffs.

4. RESTAURANT PARTNERS (/restaurant-seller)
   Dine-in restaurants, cloud kitchens, and cafes offering food menus, live
   kitchen preparation tracking, and on-demand courier dispatch.

5. DELIVERY FLEET RIDERS (/riderapp)
   Motorcycle, scooter, bicycle, and van couriers fulfilling hyper-local order
   pickups and drop-offs.

Each seller operates in an initial "RESTRICTED / PENDING" status upon account
creation. To unlock storefront publishing, item creation, and payout settlements,
the seller must complete the multi-step onboarding wizard and submit verified
KYC (Know Your Customer) documentation for manual administrative review.

2. MASTER CHECKLIST: TYPES OF DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FROM SELLERS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1. PRODUCT SELLERS DOCUMENT CHECKLIST (/product-seller)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[MANDATORY DOCUMENTS]
1. Business Registration Certificate (BRC / Trade License / Incorporation Certificate)
   - Proof of legal business existence or commercial trading permit.
   - Format: PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 4.5 MB).

2. National Identity Document (Front & Back)
   - Government-issued ID: National ID Card, Passport, Driver's License, or Voter ID.
   - Must show clear photo, full legal name, date of birth, and validity date.
   - Format: JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF (Max 4.5 MB).

3. Live Selfie with ID Card (Face Verification)
   - Live camera capture or clear headshot photo of the business owner holding
     their government ID next to their face.
   - Purpose: Proof of physical identity and anti-fraud verification.
   - Format: JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 4.5 MB).

4. User Profile Picture
   - Professional headshot of the registered account manager or owner.
   - Format: JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 4.5 MB).

5. Store Logo / Storefront Photo
   - High-resolution brand logo (1:1 square ratio recommended) or physical shop photo.
   - Format: JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 4.5 MB).

6. GST / TIN Registration Certificate (*Mandatory ONLY if seller toggles "Have GST" = Yes)
   - Official tax identification certificate issued by tax authorities.
   - Format: PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 4.5 MB).

[OPTIONAL / RECOMMENDED DOCUMENTS]
7. Store Banner Graphic
   - Horizontal banner image (16:9 or panoramic ratio) for store page header.
8. City Council / Municipal Certificate
   - Local government operational certificate or market stall clearance.
9. Proof of Business Address
   - Commercial lease agreement, utility bill (water/electricity within 3 months),
     or deed.
10. Bank Passbook / Cancelled Cheque / Bank Statement
    - Proof of bank account ownership matching the registered business/owner name.
11. Bank Verification Letter
    - Official signed and stamped letter from the bank confirming account details.

[OPTIONAL / RECOMMENDED DOCUMENTS]
8. Restaurant Hero Banner Graphic (16:9 landscape).
9. Liquor / Beverage License (if applicable).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   - Account number or mobile money number for daily/weekly delivery earnings payout.

3. DOCUMENT TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & UPLOAD POLICIES
================================================================================
To protect platform security and ensure fast page load performance, all uploads
adhere to strict automated client-side and server-side validation rules:

+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+
| PARAMETER            | SPECIFICATION / POLICY                                |
+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+
| Allowed File Types   | PDF (.pdf), JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), WebP, GIF |
| Prohibited Formats   | Word (.doc, .docx), Excel (.xls, .xlsx, .csv), SVG,   |
|                      | ZIP, RAR, EXE, BAT, scripts                           |
| Maximum File Size    | 4.5 MB per file upload                                |
| Image Compression    | Automatic in-browser compression to WebP/JPEG         |
| Security Safeguard   | SVG explicitly blocked due to script execution risks  |
| Camera Integration   | Live web camera capture modal with front/rear switch  |
| Image Cropper        | In-browser crop & rotate (1:1 square, 16:9, free)     |
+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+

> TIP: Documents must be photographed on a flat, well-lit surface with all four
  corners visible. Blurry, cropped, or glare-obscured documents will be rejected
  during the Admin KYC review stage.

4.1. STEP 1: INITIAL ACCOUNT REGISTRATION & OTP VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Route: /[seller-type]/registration -> /[seller-type]/verify-otp
Target: All seller types (Product, Service, Hotel, Restaurant, Rider)

OBJECTIVE:
Create a secure authenticated user account with verified email address and
mobile contact number.

USER ACTIONS & INPUT FIELDS:
1. First Name & Last Name (Legal name matching identity card)
2. Email Address (Used for order notifications, payouts, and admin alerts)
3. Mobile Number with International Country Code selector (e.g. +91, +232, +1)
4. Password & Confirm Password (Min 8 characters with numbers & symbols)
5. Click "Create Account"
6. Two-Factor / OTP Verification:
   - System dispatches a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) to the mobile number
     via SMS and registered email address.
   - Seller inputs the 6-digit code and clicks "Verify & Continue".

VALIDATION CHECKS:
- Email and mobile number must be unique across the platform.
- OTP expires after 10 minutes. Resend button available after 60-second cooldown.

================================================================================
5. ADMIN VERIFICATION STANDARDS & COMMON REJECTION REASONS
================================================================================
During the verification stage, platform administrators inspect submissions via
the unified Admin Portal at /admin/all-sellers using the built-in KYC Document
Viewer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CRITERIA REQUIRED FOR ADMIN APPROVAL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Identity Consistency:
   - Name on National ID / Passport must match the registered user name and bank
     account name.
2. Legibility & Quality:
   - Documents must be clear, high-resolution, uncropped, and free of glare.
   - ID numbers must be fully legible.
3. Face Verification:
   - Selfie with ID must match the person shown on the government identity card.
4. Business Validity:
   - Business Registration Certificate must have a valid registration number
     and commercial trade scope matching the chosen vertical.
5. Food Safety (Restaurants):
   - FSSAI / Health license must be current and not expired.
6. Property Proof (Hotels):
   - Ownership deed or valid lease must correspond to the listed hotel address.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOP REASONS FOR APPLICATION REJECTION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Blurry, dark, or unreadable document photographs.
- Mismatch between bank account holder name and government identity proof.
- Expired trade license, passport, or FSSAI certificate.
- Missing back side of national identity card.
- Disallowed or infringing store name (e.g. attempting to use trademarked names).
- Uploading placeholder or test files instead of authentic business documents.

6. SELLER RESUBMISSION & CORRECTION WORKFLOW
================================================================================
If an administrator rejects an onboarding application:

1. ADMIN FEEDBACK DISPATCH:
   - The admin inputs a specific, actionable rejection reason (e.g. "Your National
     ID Back photo is blurry. Please re-upload a clear copy showing the address.").
   - System sets seller status to "REJECTED" and sends an instant email notification.

2. SELLER CORRECTION PORTAL:
   - When the seller logs into their portal, an alert banner displays the exact
     Admin Feedback in red.
   - A "Fix & Resubmit Documents" button opens the specific step requiring updates.
   - The seller uploads the corrected document and clicks "Resubmit for Approval".

3. REAL-TIME ADMIN QUEUE REFRESH:
   - The seller's status transitions back to "PENDING" and reappears at the top
     of the Admin verification queue.

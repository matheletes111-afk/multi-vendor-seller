1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PLATFORM OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The MEEEM multi-vendor platform connects buyers with sellers across multiple
verticals (e-commerce products, hotel bookings, restaurant food, and home/pro services).

To guarantee buyer protection, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and seamless
automated commission accounting, all customer order payments must be held safely in
escrow and automatically split upon order delivery/completion.

This document outlines:
1. The 3-Account financial architecture.
2. The automated split-on-delivery workflow.
3. The exact banking requirements and services needed from the commercial bank.
4. The technical onboarding requirements from Flot Payment Gateway (flotme.ai).


================================================================================
2. THE 3-ACCOUNT FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
To prevent tax confusion, ensure audited escrow protection, and keep operating
income separate from customer funds, the business requires three dedicated
corporate bank accounts:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT 1: ESCROW & ORDER RECONCILIATION ACCOUNT (Holding / Transit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Purpose:
  - Receives 100% of customer payments for all product orders, hotel bookings,
    food orders, and service bookings via the Flot payment gateway.
* Behavior:
  - Funds in this account are treated as escrow/in-transit and DO NOT belong to the
    platform until services or goods are delivered.
  - If a customer cancels an unfulfilled order, refunds are issued directly from this account.
* Payout Trigger:
  - When an order status becomes "DELIVERED" or "COMPLETED", the system calculates the split:
    a) Seller Net Share is released to the seller (Bank BBAN / Mobile Money).
    b) Platform Commission Share is transferred to Account 2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT 2: PLATFORM COMMISSION & OPERATIONS ACCOUNT (Revenue / Operating)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Purpose:
  - Receives the platform's net commission cuts from fulfilled marketplace orders.
* Examples of Commission Splits:
  - Physical Products: 9% Commission (91% to seller, 9% to Account 2).
  - Hotel Bookings:   19% Commission (81% to hotel, 19% to Account 2).
  - Restaurant/Food:   Custom seller commission rate.
  - Services:          Custom service provider commission rate.
* Behavior:
  - Represents the legal gross revenue of the marketplace business for accounting,
    operational expenses, salaries, and corporate taxes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT 3: SUBSCRIPTIONS & ADVERTISING REVENUE ACCOUNT (Direct Platform Income)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Purpose:
  - Receives direct payments from registered merchants for:
    a) Monthly / Annual Seller Subscription Plans (Standard / Premium tiers).
    b) Sponsored Banner & CPC (Cost-Per-Click) Advertising Wallet Top-ups.
* Behavior:
  - These transactions do not involve third-party seller payouts or delivery escrows.
  - Customer/Seller payments for subscriptions and ads land directly in Account 3
    as immediate platform income.

3. TRANSACTION & SPLIT FLOW LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 1: CUSTOMER CHECKOUT                                                    |
| 1. Customer purchases a product (e.g. SLE 1,000) or books a hotel.           |
| 2. Customer pays via Flot (Visa/Mastercard, Flot Wallet, Mobile Money).       |
| 3. Gateway settles 100% (SLE 1,000) directly into ACCOUNT 1 (Escrow).        |
| 4. System marks Order as "PAID" / "PROCESSING". No payout is released yet.    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      |
                                      v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 2: ORDER DELIVERY / COMPLETION                                          |
| 1. Item is shipped and customer confirms delivery (or booking checked out).   |
| 2. Order status flips to "DELIVERED" / "COMPLETED".                           |
| 3. Split Calculation Engine runs:                                             |
|    - E.g. Product: SLE 910 (91%) -> Seller Net Balance                        |
|                    SLE 90  (9%)  -> Transferred to ACCOUNT 2 (Commission)    |
|    - E.g. Hotel:   SLE 810 (81%) -> Hotel Seller Net Balance                  |
|                    SLE 190 (19%) -> Transferred to ACCOUNT 2 (Commission)    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      |
                                      v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 3: SELLER DISBURSEMENT / WITHDRAWAL                                     |
| 1. Seller withdraws or system runs scheduled payout batch (e.g. weekly).      |
| 2. Payout is disbursed from ACCOUNT 1 directly to:                            |
|    - Seller's Bank Account (BBAN)                                             |
|    - Or Mobile Money Wallet (Orange Money / Africell Money)                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

       - Webhook authentication credentials (Basic Auth Username & Password).

         keep the private key secure on the production server, and provide the
         Base64-encoded Public Key to Flot Staff.

7. WHAT WE NEED TO PROCEED  (Minimum Required — Simple)
================================================================================

HOW BANK AND FLOT WORK TOGETHER:
  Flot is the tool that collects money from the customer (via card, mobile money,
  wallet). After collecting, Flot automatically sends that money to your bank account.
  So: Customer pays on Flot → Flot sends money → lands in your Bank Account 1.
  We only implement the Flot API. The bank just holds the money.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WE NEED 3 THINGS ONLY TO START:

1. FROM YOUR BANK:
   Open 3 corporate bank accounts and give us the BBAN number for each:
   - Account 1 BBAN → for customer order payments (Flot will deposit here)
   - Account 2 BBAN → for platform commission (internal transfer from Account 1)
   - Account 3 BBAN → for subscriptions & ads (Flot will deposit here directly)

2. FROM FLOT  (after you register on flotme.ai):
   - Register your business and complete KYC on Flot.
   - Give Flot your Account 1 BBAN (orders) and Account 3 BBAN (subscriptions/ads).
   - Ask Flot to give you your Merchant ID.
   - Then give that Merchant ID to the development team.

3. FROM YOU  (client decision):
   - Choose any Webhook Username and Password.
     (You share this with both Flot staff and the development team.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THAT IS ALL WE NEED. DEVELOPERS HANDLE EVERYTHING ELSE.

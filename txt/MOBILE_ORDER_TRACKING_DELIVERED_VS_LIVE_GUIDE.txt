================================================================================
MOBILE API & APP DEVELOPER GUIDE: LIVE TRACKING VS. DELIVERED ORDER MAP BEHAVIOR
PANELS: CUSTOMER MOBILE APP & SELLER MOBILE APP
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE PRINCIPLE & REQUIREMENTS (MUST-FOLLOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When an order is actively out for delivery ("ACCEPTED", "AT_PICKUP", "PICKED_UP", "OUT_FOR_DELIVERY"):
- The map must show live GPS tracking: rider's moving marker + customer destination pin + connecting route line.
- The app connects to WebSocket room `order:{orderId}` to receive live telemetry (`lat`, `lng`, `speed`, `heading`).

WHEN THE ORDER IS DELIVERED ("DELIVERED"):
- The map is STRICTLY DEDICATED to this specific order's delivery destination.
- Once delivered, DO NOT follow the rider's phone GPS anymore (the rider may be on another customer's order).
- The map MUST:
  1. Freeze/lock onto the customer's doorstep delivery destination pin (Red Pin).
  2. Hide/remove the moving rider vehicle marker.
  3. Hide/remove the polyline route.
  4. Disconnect from WebSocket room (`socketRoom` is returned as `null` by the backend).
  5. Display Status: "Delivered" and Distance: "0.0 km (Arrived)".
  6. Show action button: "Center Destination" (focuses on customer destination).

SUMMARY CHECKLIST FOR QA & MOBILE DEVS:
================================================================================
[x] During Out-For-Delivery: Rider moves in real-time via Socket.IO, polyline connects to destination.
[x] Once Delivered: WebSocket connection closed, polyline removed, vehicle marker removed.
[x] Delivered Map: Always centered on customer's delivery destination pin.
[x] Rider Doing Next Delivery: Never reflected in completed orders; completed order stays frozen at customer doorstep.
